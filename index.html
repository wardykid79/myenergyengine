The border truth

The same house, four different worlds.

Grant support for the exact same upgrade changes dramatically depending on where you live. My Energy Engine always uses the rules for your jurisdiction — and tells you plainly when you're getting the short end.

Republic of Ireland SEAI

up to €12,500

Heat-pump grant (system + central heating + €4,000 renewable-heat bonus for ditching fossil fuel). Plus attic ~€2,000, cavity ~€1,800, solar €1,800. The most generous package these islands have ever seen.

Scotland HES

up to £9,000

Home Energy Scotland — not the Boiler Upgrade Scheme. A £7,500 heat-pump grant (£9,000 rural/island), not means-tested, plus an interest-free loan for the rest and insulation funded up to 75%. Quietly the best deal in Great Britain.

England & Wales BUS

£7,500

Boiler Upgrade Scheme for an air- or ground-source heat pump — rising to £9,000 for off-gas oil/LPG homes from July 2026. Biomass £5,000 in rural off-gas homes. Insulation via means-tested ECO4.

Northern Ireland NISEP

Means-tested only

No Boiler Upgrade Scheme, no SEAI. NISEP help exists but is income-assessed (roughly under £28k single / £35k household), so many owner-occupiers qualify for little beyond 0% VAT. The honest gap we'll never hide from you.
